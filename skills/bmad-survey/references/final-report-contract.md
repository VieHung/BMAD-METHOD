# Final report contract

Write for a reader who was not present during research. The report must identify the decision context, scope, as-of date, method, source limitations, execution topology (`independent-agents`, `role-separated-single-model`, `mixed`, or `unspecified`), and publication readiness (`ready` or `blocked`) before presenting conclusions. Preserve topology per run when modes are mixed; `unspecified` blocks publication readiness.

Required sections are executive synthesis, taxonomy and coverage, technical synthesis, comparison matrix, contested or insufficient claims, limitations, open research questions, and references. Adapt subsection names to the domain rather than forcing a generic paper outline.

The comparison matrix must cover complexity, memory footprint, context or scale behavior, empirical performance, evaluation conditions, limitations, and evidence status. Do not place incomparable numbers in a shared ranking; explain the incompatibility instead.

Every central conclusion links to claim IDs. Keep supported evidence distinct from inference, and label recommendations as recommendations. Include coverage gaps and all unresolved blocking critiques. A polished narrative cannot override the workspace status.

Publication readiness requires no unsupported central claim, no hidden benchmark mismatch, no fabricated or inaccessible citation represented as verified, and no forced termination described as consensus.
