# Debate protocol

The debate operates on records shared within the authorized run: claims, evidence cards, critique records, and adjudications. Shared does not mean externally publishable; minimize source text and preserve its access restrictions. Roles never exchange hidden reasoning.

The skeptic returns critique records containing a claim ID, critique type, materiality, severity, and a concrete verification request. Useful critiques target assumptions, method, statistics, comparability, boundary conditions, missing alternatives, contradictory evidence, or source support. Rhetorical opposition cannot block a claim.

The verifier independently inspects the relevant evidence and returns the evidence checked, source locations, finding, limitations, and recommended status. It does not defend the researcher or treat confidence as support.

The adjudicator preserves the prior statement and status, answers every major critique, then records the new status, resolved and open critique IDs, rationale, and round. It may narrow a claim or add qualifiers rather than choose a false binary.

Run a further round only when open critiques are both high-impact and plausibly resolvable with available evidence. Declare `converged` when blocking critiques are resolved or stabilized as explicit disagreement. Declare `forced-termination` when a round, time, token, tool, or source limit stops the work.
