# Synthesis contract

Write for a reader who was not present during research. State decision context, scope, as-of date, evidence policy, source limitations, execution topology, and termination reason before conclusions. Preserve topology per run when modes are mixed; a later synthesis or review pass cannot upgrade `role-separated-single-model` to `independent-agents`. Missing topology is `unspecified` and blocks publication readiness.

Required content is an executive synthesis, taxonomy and coverage, technical synthesis, multidimensional comparison, contested or insufficient claims, limitations, open research questions, and references. Adapt the narrative structure to the domain.

Every central conclusion cites claim IDs. Claims marked `supported` may be stated within their recorded scope. `disputed` claims require the competing evidence. `incomparable` results remain unranked. `insufficient-evidence` and `proposed` claims are open questions, not findings. `rejected` claims may appear only to explain a correction or misconception.

Keep evidence distinct from inference and recommendations. A recommendation states its decision assumptions and the evidence status it depends on. A polished narrative never overrides workspace status.

Publication readiness requires no unsupported central claim, no hidden benchmark mismatch, no inaccessible source represented as inspected, and no forced termination described as consensus.
